[Research](http://white.stanford.edu/~brian/refs.html#reading) has shown that reading stimulates white matter growth, improves memory, and in general makes you smarter. If you think any of those things are good for you, visit [Project Gutenburg](http://www.gutenberg.org), a collection of thousands of free e-books.

Disclaimer: reading is not intended to cure any disease. Consult your physician before engaging in excessive reading. Side effects include nerdiness, myopia, and know-it-all syndrome.
